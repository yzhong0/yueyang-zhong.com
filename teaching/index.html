Teaching

I teach data analytics to Masters in Management and MBA students at London Business School and queueing theory to PhD students.

London Business School — Instructor

Data Analytics for Management

Masters in Management core · Fall 2024, 2025, 2026

Introduces the foundations of statistics, hypothesis testing, and regression analysis for managerial decision-making. Students apply these tools to management problems through hands-on projects in R, supported by AI.

Data Analytics for Managers

MBA core · Fall 2025, 2026

Develops the ability to turn data into actionable managerial insights using statistics, hypothesis testing, and regression analysis. Case studies, group projects, role play, presentations, and coding with AI agents connect analytical methods to business decisions.

Queueing Theory

PhD · Summer 2026 · Co-taught with Neil Walton, Durham University Business School

Builds a foundation in Markov chains and queueing theory, covering Lyapunov analysis, queueing games, and learning in queueing systems. Foundational lectures are complemented by research paper readings and discussions that connect core concepts to research.
